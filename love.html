03 / this part matters

love should feel like peace.

Not a guessing game. Not a test you have to keep passing. Not something that costs you your boundaries to keep.

“ You should never have to make yourself smaller to fit inside somebody else's idea of love. ”

you deserve softness and certainty, kate.

open these when you need a reminderthe little things are the big things
01Love respects your boundaries.+
Someone who truly cares for you will respect your pace, your comfort, and your decisions. You never owe anyone proof of love through something you're not ready for.
02Love doesn't punish honesty.+
You should be able to say what's bothering you without being dismissed, made to feel guilty, or afraid to bring it up again.
03Love is consistent.+
You deserve someone whose actions match their words. Someone who helps create trust rather than expecting you to ignore what feels confusing.
04Real effort is not too much to ask.+
There are people who will plan a thoughtful date, remember the tiny things, and be happy just to spend time with you. The right person won't act like basic care is a favor.
05Love leaves room for YOU.+
Your goals, values, friends, future, and peace matter. A healthy relationship adds to your life without asking you to disappear inside it.

And another thing. You do not have to prove that someone is a bad person to decide a relationship isn't right for you. How you feel and what you need are enough reasons to think carefully about your next step.
